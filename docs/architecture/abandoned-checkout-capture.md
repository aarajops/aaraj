# Abandoned checkout and staff-assisted COD order

Status: implemented in code but not deployed. The current 10-minute inactivity threshold and seven-day cleanup age are provisional implementation defaults, not approved retention policy. Do not deploy this capture flow until the business/legal owner approves its purpose, disclosure, contact basis, and retention. These settings are code constants, with no environment variables or admin settings. Scope: show abandoned checkouts beside Orders and let authorized staff place a normal COD Order after the customer confirms by phone. No automated calls, messages, recovery links, or stock holds for drafts.

## Decision

An abandoned checkout is a temporary lead, not an Order. It appears after a customer has entered a valid Bangladesh mobile number, has a nonempty cart, and has had no meaningful checkout activity for 10 minutes, whether or not the page is still open. Ten minutes is the fixed initial threshold; change it through a code release only after reviewing checkout and staff-call data. Put **Abandoned checkouts** beside Orders in the staff navigation and reuse the table layout and shared cells. Replace Order-only meanings: show a short draft ID instead of an Order reference, **Last activity** instead of **Placed**, **Estimate** instead of **COD due**, and no shipment or invoice action. A missing estimate is shown as `—`. Label each row **Abandoned checkout**; the estimate is historical, not a price promise.

The storefront's customer checkout flow stays the same, while its successful Order transaction also removes any matching draft. A staff-created Order enters the normal Orders list, inventory reservation, dispatch, Pathao, COD, and audit workflow. Successful conversion removes the draft from the abandoned list in the same database transaction. Retain the source draft ID and staff actor on the Order for traceability after deleting the draft.

## Capture and retention

1. Do not save a page visit, untouched form, incomplete phone, or empty cart. Once the phone is valid, save changed name, phone, and entered doorstep address after 1.5 seconds idle, with at least 10 seconds between ordinary saves. A quote is optional: customers who leave before choosing a delivery location still become drafts, with no quoted total yet. The selected destination is saved from the current quote when one exists.
2. Flush a snapshot on `visibilitychange` to `hidden` with a small `fetch(..., { keepalive: true })` POST. Ordinary idle saves are primary; page termination signals are best effort. Draft-service failure must not block checkout, navigation, or show a checkout error.
3. Derive ownership from the signed guest-cart cookie or customer session. The server copies current cart items and the valid quote identified by the checkout if one exists; it never accepts browser-supplied owner, items, prices, or totals. Encrypt form PII with the existing Order keyring and a distinct draft-specific authenticated-encryption domain. For a guest, also encrypt the verified guest-cart ID so an authorized conversion can coordinate with and clear that cart; do not expose it to staff.
4. Keep one draft per cart owner. Cart or quote changes update it. Reject stale autosaves with an older cart or draft version so another tab cannot overwrite newer details. Only a real form/cart change advances `last_customer_activity_at`; staff edits do not. The staff list computes eligibility from `last_customer_activity_at <= now() - 10 minutes`, so there is no abandonment-marking job or stored abandoned status. New customer activity automatically moves the row out of that list. A decline closes that owner's current draft until it expires; ignore later autosaves for the closed draft so ordinary form edits cannot reopen or extend it. A bounded cleanup worker runs every 15 minutes and deletes drafts older than seven days. Legal review must approve the actual period and contact policy.

## Staff call and Order creation

1. The list masks phone numbers. The audited detail view shows the saved items, entered address, last quoted amount when available, and last activity, with **price, delivery, serviceability, and stock unverified**. Recheck inactivity immediately before a call; the customer may have resumed checkout after staff opened the row.
2. Before calling, require a reviewed lawful basis for this specific contact purpose. Recheck that the number is not suppressed and the customer has not resumed checkout. Start the call by verifying that the person intended to shop with AARAJ before revealing items or address details. Save only the call time, staff actor, and outcome: `no_answer`, `declined`, `call_back`, `do_not_contact`, or `interested`; `call_back` also needs a future due time. A decline removes this draft from the actionable queue. A specific do-not-contact request suppresses later calls to that normalized number, including from a new cart; use a minimal keyed-hash suppression record. The suppression record persists until explicitly removed by an approved process. Do not store call recordings or free-form transcripts in this feature.
3. After `interested`, staff complete any missing delivery details. **Check current price and stock** recalculates every item from current Catalog data and the fee from current geography/tariff rules. Show the current COD amount and saved estimate when one exists. Reject unavailable items or destinations. Staff read the final items, quantities, address, delivery fee, and COD total to the customer and record agreement.
4. **Create order** requires the current preview/draft version, accepted COD amount, and recorded customer agreement. The draft UUID is its natural idempotency identity, enforced by unique `source_draft_id` on Orders; no separate staff command ledger is needed. In a short transaction, reprice and reject any amount change, revalidate the remaining facts, reserve all stock or none, persist a fresh quote and normal Order snapshots, and append audit events with staff ID, draft ID, agreement time, and accepted amount. The resulting Order can start `confirmed` and `awaiting_dispatch` because staff completed customer confirmation. An expired draft quote is never the price authority.
5. Use the same cart-owner lock and guest revision receipt/customer cart revision checks as public checkout. Every path that touches both owner and draft takes locks in that order: owner first, draft row second; public checkout already takes the owner lock before its Order insert. Reject conversion when the customer already checked out, the live cart has changed, another staff command converted it, or the preview changed. Retrying a consumed draft ID returns its existing Order; an attempted change after conversion cannot create another. Delete the draft on success inside the Order transaction. Clear an authenticated cart there too; clear a matching guest Redis cart after commit as public checkout does, with the durable guest revision receipt preventing a second Order if cleanup fails. Public checkout must also delete its matching draft in its Order transaction. Reject late browser autosaves for a consumed revision.

The existing public checkout depends on the customer's cart, a 15-minute quote, and a guest credential. Staff cannot safely call it with staff-browser cookies. Add one staff-assisted command that **reuses shared quote calculation, stock reservation, Order persistence, and audit logic**; do not duplicate pricing rules or impersonate the customer. Preserve an authenticated draft's customer ID. For a guest draft, keep its guest-cart identity and mark the Order staff-assisted. The current guest ownership database constraint requires a guest-access token, so extend it explicitly for staff-assisted guest Orders and keep the guest lookup guard closed for these Orders. Never attach a guest Order cookie to the staff browser or link an account just because its phone matches. Staff can give the Order reference on the call for the existing reference-plus-phone shipment lookup; full guest Order detail access would need a separate verified mechanism and is outside this feature. Staff fulfillment remains normal without it.

## Minimal persistence and API

One new `orders.checkout_draft` table: random `id`, unique `owner_hash`, owner kind and authenticated customer ID when present, `cart_revision`, draft version, encrypted form fields and verified guest-cart ID when present, server-derived cart/optional quote display snapshot, compact call outcome/attempt count/optional callback due time, and `last_customer_activity_at`. Index `last_customer_activity_at` for list and cleanup. Add one narrow phone suppression table only for explicit do-not-contact requests. No draft status column, event stream, CRM, or stock reservation is needed.

Add nullable `source_draft_id` (unique) and `created_by_staff_id` to Orders. Normal storefront Orders leave both null. The existing Order reference, totals, encrypted address, reservation, fulfillment, and collection fields remain authoritative.

- `POST /api/v1/checkout-drafts`: strict, small, owner-scoped autosave; current CSRF/origin protection and a 30-per-minute route limit.
- `GET /api/v1/checkout-drafts`: `checkout_drafts.manage`, `no-store`, paginated rows in the Orders table presentation; no bulk export or search initially.
- `GET /api/v1/checkout-drafts/:draftId`: `checkout_drafts.manage`, audited full-detail read.
- `POST /api/v1/checkout-drafts/:draftId/call-outcome`: `checkout_drafts.manage`, restricted and audited.
- `POST /api/v1/checkout-drafts/:draftId/preview`: `checkout_drafts.manage`, current priced preview, no stock hold.
- `POST /api/v1/checkout-drafts/:draftId/create-order`: `checkout_drafts.manage`, requires current preview and recorded customer agreement; returns the standard Order.

Reuse Orders table components, but use a distinct draft response type. A draft must never satisfy `OrderSchema` or expose Order status actions.

## Production rollout

The capture flow, staff screen, conversion path, audit records, and cleanup worker are implemented. Keep them out of production until the business/legal owner approves the capture purpose, customer disclosure/contact basis, and retention period. Then update the fixed code defaults to the approved policy and verify the end-to-end flow on the exact release candidate. No admin configuration system or separate infrastructure is needed.

No CRM, message queue, full event stream, recovery link, stock reservation on drafts, quote renewal per autosave, or separate staff idempotency ledger is required for this scope.

## Acceptance checks

- Invalid phone, untouched form, empty cart, wrong owner, oversized input, and denied origin create no draft. Repeated saves upsert one row; a missing quote still permits capture, and autosave failure leaves checkout usable.
- A draft appears after 10 minutes of inactivity, disappears from that view when active again, and is deleted after seven days. Unauthorized staff cannot read it; full-detail reads are audited.
- A call outcome alone never creates an Order. Preview shows current amounts and unavailable items; drafts never reserve stock.
- Conversion requires customer agreement to the **current** total. Price changes, sold-out variants, missing delivery details, stale previews, competing staff clicks, multiple checkout tabs, and simultaneous customer checkout cannot create duplicate or underpriced Orders.
- Successful conversion creates one normal COD Order with a fresh reservation and recorded source/actor, removes the draft atomically, and replays safely after a lost response. A delayed autosave cannot resurrect it. Failed conversion leaves the draft and stock unchanged.
- Staff-created guest Orders do not leak guest access credentials or get linked to accounts by phone. Draft PII never enters logs, analytics, URLs, or browser storage.
- Guest-to-customer cart merge consumes the guest revision with its existing durable receipt. Staff eligibility and conversion check that receipt through a Cart service method, so the old guest draft is hidden and rejected immediately; bounded draft cleanup removes its row later. This avoids a Cart-to-Orders dependency cycle. A still-open checkout may create a new customer-owned draft after sign-in.
- Staff call outcomes and detail views do not change `last_customer_activity_at`, so they cannot postpone or revive the abandonment timer. An explicit do-not-contact request suppresses later drafts for that number.

## Order-based customer directory

`/admin/customers` is a staff-only, read-only directory derived from committed Orders; it adds no customer table and does not include abandoned drafts. An authenticated account appears once its first Order is committed, with its Orders grouped by account ID. Each guest Order stays a separate entry because a recipient name or phone number is not proof that two checkouts belong to the same person. The page shows the account identity separately from the latest Order's delivery recipient, preserves cancelled and unanswered Orders in history, and uses the existing `orders.manage` permission. Customer and order-history responses are paginated and `no-store`; profile history links back to the standard Order workflow.

## Failure cases to handle without extra product machinery

- A phone number may be mistyped or belong to someone else. A saved form is not proof of phone ownership or an Order request; staff must verify the recipient and get explicit agreement before creating an Order. Do not repeatedly call `no_answer` or `declined` leads; set a simple call-attempt cap and honor declines.
- Guest cart merge on sign-in changes the cart owner. The Cart module's existing revision receipt blocks later conversion of the stale guest draft; staff eligibility hides it, and bounded cleanup removes it. Never infer that the guest phone belongs to the signed-in account.
- If the customer changes the cart, address, or destination while staff review the draft, invalidate the staff preview and require a new price and customer confirmation. If the customer completes checkout, remove the draft with that Order. Stale network saves cannot recreate it.
- If the guest cart has expired in Redis, do not silently treat the saved quote as current or recreate the cart under staff credentials. Reprice from the saved item IDs and destination, verify no Order consumed that draft/cart identity, and use the durable guest receipt when creating the Order. If those checks cannot establish a safe conversion, leave the draft for manual resolution.
- A lost response after successful conversion must return the same Order on retry. An error before commit must leave the draft and inventory unchanged. A guest Redis cleanup failure after commit must not roll back or duplicate the Order; retry cleanup separately.

## Small operational guardrails

Reuse the existing cleanup worker pattern and audit service. Track only aggregate save failures, eligible-draft count, attempted calls, conversions, and conflict/retry counts; no PII in metrics or logs. Pilot with a small staff group, check false-positive calls and wrong-number complaints, then tune the inactivity threshold and call-attempt cap. Database deletion is not the whole retention story: backup retention and any audit metadata containing draft IDs must be included in the approved policy.

## Privacy and research boundary

Outbound calls are a separate purpose from completing an active checkout. Bangladesh's [Personal Data Protection Act, 2026](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-details-1692.html?lang=en) identifies names and mobile numbers as personal data and addresses lawful basis, purpose, transparency, security, and retention in sections 5, 15, 17, and 18. Internal-only display does not remove these duties. Have local counsel approve capture, the call basis and brief disclosure/contact choice, and retention before production rollout. If customers must receive **no disclosure whatsoever**, do not use identifiable abandoned-checkout capture or outbound calling; anonymous funnel counts remain possible.

[Shopify's abandoned-checkout definition](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/abandonedcheckout) uses contact information without a completed purchase. Its [draft-order completion](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-07/mutations/draftOrderComplete) illustrates the separate draft-to-regular-Order action and inventory check. These are comparisons, not dependencies. [MDN page-lifecycle guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/sendBeacon) and [`fetch` keepalive](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Request/keepalive) support idle saves plus a best-effort page-hidden flush without an `unload` handler.
